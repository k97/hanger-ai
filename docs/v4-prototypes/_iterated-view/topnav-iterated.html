Top navigation — the segmented track

This is the specification of one control, not a comparison. Karthik ruled on 2026-08-22 in favour of the raised capsule, and the three alternatives it was drawn against — tonal, filled, and counts on the selected segment only — are gone. Structurally, the loose chips become one continuous track and the selected category is a capsule that slides inside it: position in the track says “selected”, so the check mark goes. The labels and the backend counts stay, and so does hide-at-zero with All, the selected category and MCP servers exempt (CategoryFilterCards.tsx). The slide is the app’s one spring over --dur-nav. visionOS’s glass is out — no blur. The capsule carries a tight contact shadow and no border, which is the substance of the ruling: a second, smaller elevation beside the map’s, proposed as --capsule-shadow. Click a segment, or focus one and use the arrow keys then Enter.

The same ruling settled dark. A shadow is invisible on black, and --page on --plane is one value step — so in dark the capsule lightens instead of casting a shadow nobody can see, the way iOS’s does. Its surface is a proposed token, --capsule: --page in light, --tint in dark. Flip Dark to judge.

Semantics

CategoryFilterCards renders buttons with aria-pressed (filters). Drawn as a tab view, role="tablist" / role="tab" + aria-selected fits: the list below changes with the category. Arrow keys move between tabs; the indicator follows focus only on selection.

Carried over / changed / dropped
  • Carried over: the labels, the backend counts, the hide-at-zero rule, the h-7 px-3.5 rounded-pill geometry, the strip below.
  • Changed: one track instead of loose chips, with a capsule that slides to the selection; the check mark goes, because position now carries it. The capsule has no border and a contact shadow (ruled 2026-08-22) — two proposed tokens, --capsule and --capsule-shadow, the latter a second elevation beside the map’s.
  • Dropped: glass, blur, visionOS’s hover-expand (pointer, not gaze). Also the three capsule treatments this page was drawn to compare — tonal, filled, and count-on-selected-only — all ruled out on 2026-08-22.
