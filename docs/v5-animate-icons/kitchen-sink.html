Animated icons — state kitchen sink

Every empty, pending and loading state in the app, in one page, at true size. Geometry is Lucide (ISC, already a dependency); motion is CSS, re-implemented in _proto.css — no motion package, and prefers-reduced-motion removes all of it for free. Colours, sizes, radii and beats come from the running app’s own tokens.css, so light and dark are real.

To swap a mark: replace the inline <svg> paths for that state and keep its motion class. The class vocabulary is what ports into index.css later — the geometry is what you are choosing here.

loop Active — something is happening. Runs until it stops.
once Resting — a finding. Plays once, then holds still.
current The mark that ships today.
proposed A change to an existing mark.
new This state has no icon at all today.

Size ladder — the constraint that decides family 5

Only the sizes animated sites actually use, counted from the tree: 12 (5 sites), 13 (4), 32 (1, app boot), 40 (4, the planes). Judge at 100% zoom.

11 is an outlier — exactly one animated site uses it, CategoryFilterCards.tsx:110. It is drawn below the rule for comparison only; moving that site to 12 retires the size and costs nothing. The one other size-11 icon in the app (DiscoveryPane.tsx:109) is a static hover glyph, not animated.

The finding this ladder exists for: one mark cannot serve both 40 and 12. disc-3’s two arcs and 2-unit hub collapse into the rim below about 16; loader-circle is a single arc and holds. That argues for a pair — disc-3 on the planes, loader-circle inline — not one mark stretched across both.

Element count is its own limit (Karthik, 2026-08-24). radar was dropped from every candidate strip: 8 elements read fine as a static mark and turn to mush the moment they move at 12–13. As a rough rule, a mark that animates at inline sizes wants 4 elements or fewer — disc-3 is 4, server is 4, loader-circle is 1. Denser marks stay static, or stay on the 40 planes.

12 vs 13 is not noise. 13 is where action buttons sit (Rescan in SummaryStrip, NeedsReviewPane; Verify in McpServerDetail); 12 is inline status beside text. One site breaks it: RepoPane.tsx:407 is a button at 12. Worth deciding whether to keep the split and fix that site, or collapse both to one size.

What ports into the app

The class vocabulary above is the deliverable, not the HTML. It moves into src/styles/index.css as @utility rules beside animate-drop / -rise / -tip, and src/components/icons.tsx grows one wrapper beside sized(). Marks keep passing through sized(), so strokeFor() and the optical table still apply — these prototype SVGs hardcode stroke-width: 2 only because they are not going through it here. See 00-state-inventory.md for the per-site mapping and the open questions.
