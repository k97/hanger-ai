Global › the top of the page: seven layouts

The tabs, the engine selector and the summary at the top of Global. Every frame is the real window size (1024 × 640) with the details panel open, the width where today's chips wrap to three rows. Values are Hanger's light theme. The engine marks are placeholders, not the real logos.

The goal: pick a kind and an engine quickly, keep the cost figure readable, and give the list most of the height.

A · Today baseline

Tabs, then ten chips over three rows, then a summary card. Picking an engine takes one click, but the scope controls and the summary use about 330 px before the first row of the list.

B · Engine menu recommended

The kinds and the engine share one row. The engine is a pop-up button, as in Xcode's scheme picker or Mail's mailbox menu. Its menu lists every engine with its cost, so opening it also compares them: you see at once which engine is heaviest. You can type to filter, use ↑↓ and Return, and open it with ⌘E. The summary shrinks from four lines to two. The list starts about 150 px higher than today.

F · Overview card, from the window bar new · after T3

The page keeps only the kind tabs, so the list starts right under them. Everything about scope and cost moves into one card that drops from a button at the right of the window bar, the way T3's session card does. The card has three parts. Engines ranks every engine by its cost, with a thin meter bar each, and clicking one picks it. The chosen engine shows its total and one meter for each kind, with dashed bars for kinds that aren't measured yet. Actions holds Needs review and Rescan. The breadcrumb names the engine you're looking at. The card opens instantly, ⌘E toggles it, and Esc closes it.

G · The same card, pinned new · after T3

The second window-bar button pins the card into the right column, like T3's pinned session panel. That's the column's resting state when nothing is selected, which today is an empty "Details panel". Selecting an asset shows its details in that column, and Esc or deselecting brings the overview back. Tick marks to the left of the list stand for its groups (Skills, MCP servers, Rules, Subagents, Switched off, Not loaded). The dark tick is the group in view. Hover a tick for its name and count, and click it to jump there.
