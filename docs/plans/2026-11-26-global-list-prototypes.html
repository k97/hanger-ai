Drawn from T3's source (/Users/karthik/Work/Labs/t3code/apps/web/src), not from its screenshots. Every frame is your dev window's size, 1024 × 700. Values are Hanger's light theme. The engine marks are placeholders.

T3's rules used here:

  • Rows, not tables. Each row has an icon, a name that truncates, and one trailing slot on the right that shows either the state or the hover action. There are no column headers.
  • Second-line details. Secondary information goes on an optional muted second line.
  • Fixed sizes. List rows are 36 px, group headers 32 px, and rows sit 1 px apart.
  • Quiet group headers. A muted label, a hairline, then the count.
  • A centred content column. Content sits in a column of at most 736 px, with 48 px padding and tick marks in its left margin.
  • A floating card. The session card is 280 px wide and floats at the top right. The content column moves left to keep 32 px clear of it, and the card becomes a popover when there isn't room.
  • Thin meters. Meters are 3 px with a tick at the end, in 24 px rows.
